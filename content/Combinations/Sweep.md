It is a combination of:

- [[Cross Body Lead]]
- Flick the followers left arm (so leaders right) underneath the other arm and grab it
- [[Right Turn]] while holding both hands, but keeping your left arm on neck height
- Turn the lady next to you
- 3 steps backwards
- Throw the front hand to the back and switch hands
- [[Right Turn]]
- [[Basic Step]] on the stop while doing a [[Hair Comb]]
- Prepare a turn, but move the other direction on 6.

From here on, there are complicated moves that I couldn't really do. Just go to regular basic again.
