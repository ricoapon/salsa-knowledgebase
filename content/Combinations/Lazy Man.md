Personal note: this move where you throw the hand of the follower into a [[Hammerlock]] never works in socials. I wouldn't do this. Also, I don't like to stand with my back to the follower, having no visible connection.

## Steps

1. [[Open Break]] where you throw the hand higher than the shoulder, so the follower goes into a [[Hammerlock]]
1. [[Outside Turn]] and step in front of the follower with your back to the follower
1. [[Open Break]] and flick the arm of the follower to turn her for the next turn
1. [[Inside Turn]]
