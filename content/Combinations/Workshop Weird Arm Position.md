This is the move from the workshop that does the following:
- [[Right Turn]] for the follower, where the leader does not let go of any hands. Now the follower ends up in a position with one arm in the elbow of the other.
- Arm flick and catch the arm. Throw the arm on the back into hammerlock.
- [[Cross Body Lead Outside Turn]], with the same hand position as before.
- [[Open Break]] into [[Copa]], but with different hand hold.
