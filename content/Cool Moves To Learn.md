This page contains videos with moves that I found that I think are really nice to learn. There is no order to this list.

# Cross Body Lead Rejection Hook into follower walking around you

https://www.instagram.com/reel/C5xpeHfiqRf/

Instagram doesn't have a timestamp. Somewhere at the end he does a [[Cross Body Lead Rejection Hook]], but he doesn't switch the hands. He leads the follower on the side and moves her back, going into [[Cross Body Lead]] again. Should not be that difficult.

# Walking backwards in a circle together

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/gsI1L6_7N8E)

A bit tricky with the steps, especially if followers are not prepared for this. Tricky in the timing when to lead the follower out of it.
