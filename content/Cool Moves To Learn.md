This page contains videos with moves that I found that I think are really nice to learn. There is no order to this list.

# Open Break Outside Turn

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/OU0RR88dDBs)

Something to do on an open break!

# Exit Enchufla Doble

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/dMpR5nk_5sw?start=45)

There are many ways to exit the [[Enchufla Doble]], like the CBL that I normally do. This is a cool one with a turn for myself.

# Cross Body Lead Rejection Hook into follower walking around you

https://www.instagram.com/reel/C5xpeHfiqRf/

Instagram doesn't have a timestamp. Somewhere at the end he does a [[Cross Body Lead Rejection Hook]], but he doesn't switch the hands. He leads the follower on the side and moves her back, going into [[Cross Body Lead]] again. Should not be that difficult.

# Walking backwards in a circle together

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/gsI1L6_7N8E)

A bit tricky with the steps, especially if followers are not prepared for this. Tricky in the timing when to lead the follower out of it.

# Double handed turns

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/PFK1t8d0Zgo)

Just looks very cool haha. Also doable with beginners if I lead well maybe?

# Barrel roll

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/E6ofN0HpI6Q?start=159)

A bit too difficult. But maybe with some practise I could do this? The distance between the follower and leader is tight, so not suited for a beginner.

# Wrap with complete turn

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/E6ofN0HpI6Q?start=134)

Now that I have learned [[Shoulder Check Outside Turn]], I can do this too probably! Feels a bit similar.
