---
title: Wrap Outside Turn
tags:
  - learned
---

Do a [[Wrap]], but instead of turning the follower back, step with the follower forwards. Turn together, so you end up next to each other. Make sure to twist the follower inwards, so you prepare for an [[Outside Turn]].
