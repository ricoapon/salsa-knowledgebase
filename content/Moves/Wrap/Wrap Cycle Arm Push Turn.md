Do a [[Wrap]]. On 5, cross-step behind your leg. You want to move forwards and then to the side. But the follower is in the way, so you pull her backwards. Now make sure to keep the followers arm low, so you step into the arm. When the follower steps forwards, your back will push against her arm, making the follower turn.
