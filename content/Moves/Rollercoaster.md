---
title: Rollercoaster
tags:
  - learned
---

Start with a [[Reverse Crosshold]]. Do a [[Cross Body Lead Inside Turn]] and immediately go into a new [[Cross Body Lead]] with a half turn by the leader. Now you end up in the same position as you started with.

You can repeat this move or you can go into something like a [[Butterfly]].
