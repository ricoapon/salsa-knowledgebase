title: Copa Low Check
tags:
  - learned
---

The normal [[Copa]], but keep the hand low. This way, the follower cannot keep turning (resulting in a check). Return with an [[Outside Turn]].

The combination in the video shows:

- [[Cross Body Lead Hand Switch]] and grab the hand for [[Crosshold]]
- [[Right Turn]] to [[Reverse Crosshold]]
- [[Copa Low Check]]
- [[Outside Turn]] with touch-and-go to switch the hands
- [[Left Turn]] for the leader
