This is a variant of the [[Cross Body Lead]] where you start with a [[Crosshold]]. The leader makes the regular [[Cross Body Lead]] steps, but some additional hand movement on 1 and 2. On 1, the leader does a [[Hair Comb]] using the right hand over the head of the follower. Then on 2, the leader does a [[Hair Comb]] using the left hand over the head of the leader. Then finish the regular [[Cross Body Lead]].

![YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/6OW1gkSE2d8?start=120)

An alternative way of doing it after a [[Cross Body Lead Inside Turn]] is to keep the arm raised and then lower it onto your neck.

![Beginners 2 Lesson 4](https://youtu.be/r64V_aR5Lqc?start=28)

You can do the same move as the leader, but turning yourself to the right (so you make a 270 degree turn). This is possible, but takes more practise to end up correctly. You also don't see the follower, so with a less experienced follower, you could end up in a different position than expected. The move from the video is a easier and less error-prone.
