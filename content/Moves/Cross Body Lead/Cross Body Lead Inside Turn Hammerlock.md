This is a variant of the [[Cross Body Lead Inside Turn]] where you go into a [[Hammerlock]] position.

Start with a [[Reverse Crosshold]]. Do the [[Cross Body Lead Inside Turn]], but make sure to keep to move the right hand low on 5 and 6 and keep the arm of the follower straight. The follower will turn into a [[Hammerlock]] position.
