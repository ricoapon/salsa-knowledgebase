---
title: Cross Body Lead Outside Turn Hammerlock
tags:
  - learned
---

While doing a [[Cross Body Lead Outside Turn]], use the [[Right To Left]] hand hold. Then move the right hand over the followers head and quickly all the way down. This makes the follower turn into the hand and land in a [[Hammerlock]].

It is logical to follow it up with an [[Open Break]], so something like a [[Copa]], as is done in the video.
