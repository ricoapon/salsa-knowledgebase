---
title: Turn Into Roll In Check
tags:
  - learned
---

Start with a [[Handhold]] and do an [[Open Break]]. Pull the follower in to turn her to be in front of you. Keep turning until you are in a similar position as [[Roll In Check]] (only difference is that you don't do the 3 steps backwards). Do a check and return with an [[Outside Turn]].
