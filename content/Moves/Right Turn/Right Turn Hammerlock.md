---
title: Right Turn Hammerlock
tags:
  - learned
---

Do a [[Right Turn]] with [[Parallel Hold]] and keep holding both hands. The leader should keep the left hand high, but the right hand low. This makes the follower go into a [[Hammerlock]].

Note that the video below shows a [[Hammerlock Into Copa Back To Back]], not just going into a [[Hammerlock]].
