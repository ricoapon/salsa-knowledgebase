---
title: Open Break Inside Turn
tags:
  - learned
---

Start with [[Right To Left]] and do an [[Open Break]]. Turn the follower slightly to the outside and present your left hand. This signals already to the follower that the turn can only go one way. Continue with a turn.

A logical followup is the [[Rollercoaster]].
